## 1. airplanes.live at Rome Fiumicino, July 2026 — dark like adsb.lol

adsb.lol has no ground point at LIRF in July 2026 (10,000–18,000 a day in January 2026; `tools/adsb_extract_day.py` extracts). The
18 September probe of airplanes.live's `globe_history` looked at LFPG and LTFM only. This probe fetched the airplanes.live trace of
the 40 lowest-altitude aircraft that adsb.lol saw in the LIRF box on each of four days (`airplaneslive_lirf_probe_20260930.csv`):

| day | aircraft | with any ground point in the box | with 20 or more | ground points |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 15 January 2026 | 40 | 39 | 34 | 2,921 |
| 3 July 2026 | 40 | 12 | 0 | 47 |
| 15 July 2026 | 40 | 3 | 0 | 6 |
| 22 July 2026 | 40 | 3 | 0 | 37 |

In July an aircraft that has ground points has one or two minutes of them (a stray receiver reaching the runway), never the
pushback. Both networks lost the same surface feed at Fiumicino; a match needs the first surface point at the stand. **Closed.**
airplanes.live's terms are non-commercial (as EUROCONTROL's); the question did not arise.

## 2. UK CAA punctuality statistics (Heathrow) — real, small

`https://www.caa.co.uk/data-and-analysis/uk-aviation-market/flight-punctuality/uk-flight-punctuality-statistics/2026/` publishes,
per month, airport, airline and origin/destination, the number of matched flights, the average delay in whole minutes (early = 0) and
the shares of flights in ten delay bands (more than 15 min early … more than 360 min late). Delay is actual off-block against the
planned gate time (Airport Coordination Ltd or the airport, changes up to 30 minutes before operation). All-cargo, positioning and
private movements are excluded. July 2026 is published (run date 22 September 2026). Heathrow departures: 19,116 matched flights in
January 2026 and 20,430 in July 2026, in 326 / 345 airline-route groups. Terms on the page: "No statistical data provided by CAA may
be sold on to a third party. CAA insists that they are referenced in any publication that makes reference to CAA Statistics."
Files: `data/external/caa_punctuality/` (not in the repository).

It is the same kind of aggregate as v48's source (a sum of max(off-block − schedule, 0)) at a different grain: airline × route × month
instead of airport × day, and its mean is rounded to the minute (±30 s per flight) where the daily total is exact to the minute over
the day. What it could fix is the group-level bias of our Heathrow predictions. Ceiling on the development months, v48's procedure as
the base (`n·mean² − noise floor` over the groups, before any source noise):

| month | airline × destination, share of EGLL squared error | share of the month's squared error |
|---|---:|---:|
| January 2025 | 1.25% | 0.054% |
| July 2025 | 0.75% | 0.090% |
| November 2025 | 0.99% | 0.096% |
| December 2025 | 1.00% | 0.128% |

That is at most 0.1% of a month's squared error with perfect group means, about 0.1 s of RMSE, before the minute rounding and the
shrinkage a real procedure needs; a working procedure might keep a third. The mapping of airline names and destination names to the
supplied codes is a few hours of work. **Parked: real but too small for the effort while anything larger is open.**

## 3. FlyItalyADSB — the only path left to Fiumicino's July 2026 surface

FlyItalyADSB (Italian network, data CC BY-SA 4.0, attribution "FlyItalyADSB (flyitalyadsb.com) · ADS-B/MLAT data · Infrastructure:
Nesso Labs (nessolabs.it)") runs a REST API (`https://api.flyitalyadsb.com/v2`, key issued by e-mail registration) with a live
area query and a history endpoint that is self-serve for recent days only. Older data "is available for university or institutional
research upon request" (`https://flyitalyadsb.com/dati/archivio`). The site refuses unauthenticated reads. Whether it has ground
coverage at Fiumicino cannot be told without a key (a live area query would show it). If it does and July 2026 could be obtained,
the LIRF July 2026 rows (all 15,800 unmatched; the largest block of the projected ranking error) could be matched like 2025's.
This needs a registration and a request to a third party: the user's decision.
